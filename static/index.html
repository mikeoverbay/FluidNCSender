Connect to FluidNC

Nothing is sent until you press Connect. Then FluidSender asks FluidNC for its status, version ($I) and modes ($G), reads the axes' max speeds and accelerations and the coolant outputs' pins ($/axes/…, $/coolant/…; it never changes them) and the spindle type ($SS), and switches to inches (G20) once the machine is Idle; G-code you send before that waits for it. It never homes, unlocks, resets or moves by itself. A port another program holds (like UGS) can't be chosen.
Bluetooth jog pendant

The pendant talks over Bluetooth: Blueman makes its link /dev/rfcomm1 (in Blueman: the pendant → Serial Port). Nothing is opened until you press Connect. Then FluidSender answers the pendant and sends it the position, the state and the job's progress a few times a second. Its keys jog with the Jog panel's step and speed (its step keys change them there too), zero X / Y / Z, go to zero, home, run, pause / resume and stop, like the buttons here; the Console shows each key. If the pendant stops answering for a while it shows “waiting”, and goes on by itself when it answers again. Switching the pendant off drops Blueman's Bluetooth link: it then shows “off”, and you connect it here again once it is on (FluidSender never connects by itself).

ConsoleEnter sends the line to FluidNC · Up / Down: earlier lines · ? ! ~ on their own go at once (status, feed hold, resume)
Jog buttonsClick: one step of the chosen distance (10 … 0.001 in) · Hold: moves until you let go · Stop: stops at once
Jog keysWhile the Jog panel has focus (click it; it shows “keys on”): ← → X · ↑ ↓ Y · PageUp / PageDown Z · a tap is one step, holding moves until you let go · Esc stops
Two clicksZero (X, Y, Z), Zero All, Undo Zero, Go to XY 0 and Go to Z 0 ask first: the first click turns the button amber (“Zero X? click again”; Go to says how far and which way it moves, and if Z goes up first), a second click 0.5 to 3 s later does it. Any key (Esc too), a click anywhere else or waiting 3 s cancels. A double-click never counts: wait half a second before the second click
Mouse onlyNo key presses the buttons that move the machine, set a zero or switch an output: Run, Resume, Home, Go to, Zero, the jog pad, Start Scan, Home Probe, Vacuum and AUX, the overrides (Tab skips them, and clicking them leaves the keys with the Jog panel). Hold, Soft Reset and Stop work from the keyboard too
PositionClick a work position to type a new one (Enter sets it; Enter on the value as it was sends nothing; Esc or clicking elsewhere cancels) · Undo Zero puts the last zero back, and again for the one before: the last 5 zeros (from the page or the pendant) are kept, also through a reset, a reconnect or a restart
Go to XY 0 / Z 0Rapid moves to the work zero. Go to XY 0 first lifts Z to 0.2 in if it is lower
Vacuum / AUXToggle the vacuum (M8) and the AUX relay (M7), at once, also during a job. They follow FluidNC's own M7 / M8 / M9 state (what $G says), so a program's M8 / M7 / M9 shows too, once FluidNC has run it. A toggle is refused, with the reason on the button, while the program's own M8 / M7 / M9 still waits for the moves before it (a click then would cancel it), while jogging, and when FluidNC has no pin for that output (“no pin”). With a coolant delay set in FluidNC, switching on during a job holds up its planning that long (the button's tooltip says it). Not connected, they show the last state seen with a ?: “on?” in amber may still be on, since disconnecting switches nothing off (Disconnect asks first when one is on)
▶ Run · ⏸ Hold · ResumeButtons whose labels never change (their tooltips say what each does now, or why not). ▶ Run (green when it can run) starts the loaded G-code from the top: it asks first, and says the units and modes it runs in (FluidSender sends G20 G90 first, so a program without units runs in inches) and whether it is LEVELED (or NOT leveled, when a scan exists); the editor is read-only while it runs and follows the line being run · ⏸ Hold (feed hold) slows down to a stop · Resume continues after a Hold, an M0 pause or an error pause (a job goes on with the next line). Hold and Resume are off in an ALARM. M1 does nothing in FluidNC (3.9.4 and 4.1.1). Lines FluidNC runs otherwise than they read are shown as warnings when the file is loaded (they still run): an M7 or M8 whose line ends in a number like F100.1 turns AUX or the vacuum OFF, so put M7 / M8 on a line of its own. FluidNC 4.1.1 refuses G-code lines over 127 characters (FluidSender says which line when the file is loaded)
OverridesMachine panel, under the job: Feed, Spindle (S) and Rapid, at once, also during a job or a hold (FluidNC's realtime overrides; mouse only). Feed: the cutting moves (G1, G2, G3), 10 to 200 % in steps of 1 and 10, or back to 100 %. Spindle: the S the same way; under it the S now (and the programmed S). An on/off spindle only changes the S reported, and below 100 % a small S can switch it off. Rapid (G0): 100, 50 or 25 %. A value not at 100 % is amber; the Console says each new value once FluidNC reports it. A reset and M2 / M30 put them back to 100 % (so does Machine → Overrides to 100%)
Soft ResetNo job: Ctrl-X at once. During a job or a scan: stops it safely (Hold first, then the reset once the machine has stopped, so the position is kept). An ALARM leaves the spindle, the vacuum and AUX on: the Machine panel then says so in red, and Soft Reset (red too) turns them off
ScanThe Scan panel sits under the visualizer, left of the Console (View → Scan puts it back there), in columns: Probe | Grid | Scan | Auto-level. Connect the probe, Home Probe, set the area (or Use G-code area), Start Scan. The points show in the visualizer as they come; Save Scan… writes the .xyz file. A Stop, an abort, or a Home that fails (Home Probe, or the scan's retract) leaves the probe not homed, its tip maybe down: the Scan and Jog panels say so (“Probe may be down”) until it is homed again, also once the probe is disconnected or let go of after the window closed (only a restart of FluidSender's server, after an update or a reboot, forgets it). They say so for a probe connected and not homed yet too, and still once the window closed and let go of it. Connect the probe if it is not, and Home Probe before moving X or Y: it is a warning only, X and Y moves are not refused
Auto-levelThe Scan panel's last column: Level the G-code with the last scan (or a .xyz file), the Z surface (0: the G-code's Z 0 is the scanned surface) and the arc segment (arcs become straight pieces this long; 0.1 mm). It levels the way UGS does. From then on Run (and the pendant's Run) runs the leveled G-code: the editor shows it read-only under a banner (Show original, Save leveled G-code…, Leveling off), the visualizer draws it with the scan's area dashed and any moves outside it pink (they get the nearest edge's height), and Run asks with “LEVELED with …”. Opening or changing the G-code, a new scan or another FluidNC turns leveling off (the Console says so); it is never done again by itself. Moving the table or the X / Y zero after a scan (Y shifted for the probe, then put back) never blocks Level or Run: they say by how much it moved. Z must be zeroed on the surface at the scan's first point (FluidSender warns when it can tell)
WindowsA window opened (or reloaded) with no G-code shows the program FluidSender has loaded (during a job, the job's), the one the pendant's Run runs. A window whose G-code is not the loaded one (another window opened another file) says so in amber under Run; its Run loads its own G-code first
PendantMachine → Connect Pendant… (/dev/rfcomm1). Its keys jog with the Jog panel's step and speed, zero, go to zero, home, run, pause / resume and stop; the status bar shows “Pendant: connected”. A jog key moves 1 in at most (with the 10 in step it moves 1 in), and a held key never gets more than one step ahead of the machine. Its Run resumes a paused job (as Resume does), and starts the loaded program when FluidNC is Idle; neither Run nor Pause resumes a job an error paused (that is told and resumed here). The pendant's own firmware decides when Run, Pause and Stop are sent. With the fixed firmware (NANO_jogbox.ino of 2026-10) they follow the machine as its screen shows it (ALARM, STOPPING, ERROR … too): Stop always, Pause unless IDLE, Run in IDLE with a program loaded or to resume a pause, and a double tap counts within 1 s. With the original firmware, Pause and Stop send nothing unless Run was pressed on the pendant (so for a job or a scan started here the status bar says “Pendant Stop/Pause inactive”: use Hold or Soft Reset), after a Run it sends Run again only after its Stop key (a refused Run says so in the Console), and the double-tap keys (zero, go to zero, home) have no time limit, so a single tap stays armed until the next tap of that key. Switching the pendant off drops its Bluetooth link: the status bar then shows “Pendant: off”; connect it again from the Machine menu. Closing FluidSender's window lets go of the machine, the pendant and the probe about 10 s later, once FluidNC is at rest and answering (Idle, or an ALARM with the spindle and vacuum/AUX off). Never during a job or a scan: then the pendant stays, for its Stop and Pause. A Hold, an open door, an ALARM that may have left the spindle or vacuum/AUX on (until a Soft Reset turns them off: after a hard or soft limit the pendant's Stop is that reset) or another FluidSender window still open keeps all three. The next window connects none of them by itself: connect FluidNC, the pendant and the probe again. The pendant's Home all ($H) works only in ALARM (to home otherwise, use Home here), and Machine X0 / Y0 (G53) are refused while Z is under 0.2 in above the work zero: lift Z first (Machine Z0)

About FluidSender

FluidSender: a lean G-code sender for FluidNC, built for this machine.

Dockable panels, G-code editor, 3D toolpath and scan view, the FluidNC connection over USB with live position, state and console (stage 1); jog, home, unlock and zero (stage 2); running jobs with Run, Hold, Resume and a safe Soft Reset, the vacuum (M8) and AUX (M7) toggles, Go to XY 0 / Z 0 and two-click zeroing (stage 3); the Bluetooth jog pendant, the Leonardo Z probe and the surface scan (stage 4); FluidNC 4.1.1 (3.9.4 still works), the feed / spindle (S) / rapid overrides, auto-leveling the G-code with the scan as UGS does it, the Scan panel beside the Console, ▶ Run · ⏸ Hold · Resume · Soft Reset with labels that never change, the vacuum and AUX following FluidNC's M7 / M8 state, and the pendant's Run, Pause and Stop following the machine with its fixed firmware (stage 5).
